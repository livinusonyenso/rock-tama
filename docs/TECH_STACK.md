### `next.config.ts` flags [CONFIRMED present]

- `cacheComponents: true`: Cache Components / Partial Prerendering model. Implications: pages are prerendered by default; **dynamic or non-deterministic work** (`cookies()`, `headers()`, `new Date()`, `Math.random()`, uncached fetches) must be wrapped in `<Suspense>` or opted into caching with `"use cache"`. This matters for the cart badge and the footer copyright year.
- `partialPrefetching: true` and `experimental.agentFeedback: true`: generated by create-next-app. Leave as is unless a problem appears.
- `turbopack.rules["*.css"]`: Tailwind loader.
