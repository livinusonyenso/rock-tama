Individual retail buyers · hotels and hospitality · offices and workplaces · supermarkets and retail distributors · wholesale/bulk purchasers · international export customers.

## Business objectives [CONFIRMED]

1. Establish a premium online brand presence.
2. Showcase natural spring water products.
3. Support online shopping and product discovery.
4. Generate wholesale and distributor enquiries.
5. Present the water's origin, purity and quality information.
6. Support customer engagement through contact channels.
7. Build a scalable foundation for future e-commerce capabilities.

## Content that needs business evidence before publication [ASSUMPTION]

The reference contains product and quality claims. They are **supplied visual content, not verified facts**. Each must be backed by documentation held by the business before the site goes live:

| Claim (as shown in the reference) | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| "Ultra Pure", "Pure by Nature" | Marketing language. Confirm the owner is comfortable with it alongside lab data. |
| "Bottled at Source" | Confirmation that bottling happens at the spring site. |
| "Low TDS (6–8 mg/L)" | Current laboratory analysis report showing the TDS range. |
| "Heavy Metals Not Detected" | Lab report covering heavy-metal panel and detection limits. |
| "Pathogens Absent" | Microbiological lab report. |
| "Lab Tested" / "rigorously tested" | Name/accreditation of the testing lab, test frequency. |
| "Undistilled (Natural State)" (reference reads "Unstilled") | Process confirmation; spelling to be corrected. |
| "Odourless & Tasteless at Source" | Sensory/lab evidence. |
| "Naturally protected source", "filtered through ancient rock formations" | Hydrogeological survey or similar. |
| "ISO 22000" badge and "Manufactured under ISO 22000 Food Safety Management" | **Valid ISO 22000 certificate** (certifying body, number, expiry). Do not display a badge without it. |
| "International quality standards" | Which standards specifically. |
| "Nationwide Delivery", "Export Supply", "International Enquiries Welcome" | Confirmation that these services exist operationally. |
| Regulatory registration (e.g. NAFDAC number for packaged water in Nigeria) | Not shown in the reference; ask whether it should appear (common on packaged-water sites). |
